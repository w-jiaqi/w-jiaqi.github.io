Jiaqi Wang

I studied Computer Science, Applied & Computational Mathematical Sciences (ACMS), and Philosophy at the University of Washington.

I was affiliated with the UW Natural Language Processing group, where I worked with Noah Smith, and the Institute for Protein Design, where I worked with David Baker. I also worked with Eric Xing at Carnegie Mellon University.

Email  /  Google Scholar  /  GitHub  /  Twitter

Research

I work on developing generative models for scientific discovery. My current research focuses on machine learning for biomolecular design and drug discovery, with broader interests in multimodal learning, natural language processing, and foundation models.
